## Handoff Notes

- **For idea optimization:** Separate communication, authority, and execution
  topology. Preserve the matched executable endpoint and avoid another
  topology-aware attack.
- **For experiment design:** Primary graph comparisons must hold fixed ex-ante
  calls, tokens, delivered message slots, tool permissions, and action budgets.
- **For writing:** Lead with the architecture-bundling problem and the
  \(G_C/G_A/G_E\) decomposition, not with a generic statement that topology
  matters.
- **For review:** MasDrift is the strongest authorization overlap;
  Architecture Matters is the strongest broad empirical overlap.
